# Good GYM Terms of Use

Effective date: November 5, 2026.

1. **Who can use the app.** People aged 16 and over. By creating a profile you confirm your age and accept these terms and the privacy policy.
2. **Account.** One person, one account. You are responsible for keeping your password safe and for actions in your account.
3. **Records.** A record enters the leaderboard only after a moderator reviews its video. The video must show one full set, the bar and plates, with no edits or cuts. A moderator may reject a record with a reason; a rejection can be appealed once.
4. **Not allowed:** submitting someone else's videos or fake results, insulting or harassing other users, posting illegal or explicit content, bypassing service limits or protection.
5. **Reports and blocking.** You can report a user or a record and block a user. The administration may remove content and close an account for breaking these terms.
6. **Coach and student.** A coach sees a student's data only while a link confirmed by both sides is active. Either side can end the link at any time.
7. **Health.** The app does not give medical advice. Train according to your condition and consult a doctor if in doubt. You train at your own risk.
8. **Your content.** Videos and texts remain yours. You allow us to store them and show them to moderators and users within your privacy settings until you delete them or your account.
9. **Service as is.** The app is free and provided without guarantees of uninterrupted operation. We may change features and these terms; material changes will be announced in the app.
10. **Termination.** You can delete your account at any time: Settings → Account → Delete account.
11. **Contact.** The Issues section of https://github.com/meloton01/Good-GYM.
