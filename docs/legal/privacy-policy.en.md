# Good GYM Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026.

## Who processes the data

Good GYM is developed and maintained by the owner of the repository https://github.com/meloton01/Good-GYM. Questions and data requests: the Issues section of that repository.

Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited) stores and processes data on our behalf: Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, App Check.

## What we collect

| Data | Why | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Email, password (hashed by Firebase), Google or Apple sign-in | Signing in, password reset | Only you |
| Nickname, role, bio, gym | Profile and gym leaderboard | People you allow in privacy settings |
| Body weight and height | Weight class in the leaderboard | Only you. A record stores only the weight class |
| Workouts, templates, notes | Workout log and progress charts | You and your coach while the link is active |
| Coach link, plans, comments | Coach and student work | Both sides while the link is active |
| Records and lift videos | Moderator review and leaderboard | Moderators; an approved record is visible to people allowed to see your records. Videos are served by a link valid for 15 minutes |
| Reports and blocks | Community safety | You; reports are reviewed by the administration |
| Consent time, age confirmation | Legal consent requirement | Administration only |
| App settings (language, theme, units) | Interface | Stored on your device |
| App Check token | Protection against fake clients | Processed by Google |

We do not use ads, behavioral analytics or cross-app tracking. We do not sell or share data with third parties other than Google Firebase as a processor.

## Device permissions

- Camera: scanning invitation QR codes and recording lift videos.
- Microphone: sound in lift videos.
- Photo library: choosing an existing lift video.
- Notifications and exact alarms: rest timer alert. Notifications are created on the device.

## Legal basis

Performance of the contract (terms of use) for the account, workouts and leaderboard; your consent given when creating a profile; legitimate interest in service safety for reports, blocks and App Check.

## Age

The app is for people aged 16 and over. A profile cannot be created without confirming the age.

## Retention

Data is kept while the account exists. Deleting the account in the app (Settings → Account → Delete account) removes the profile, workouts, plans, coach links, records with videos, blocks, reports you filed and the sign-in account. Firebase backups are purged on Google's schedule.

## Your rights

You can get a copy of your data (Settings → Account → Export data), correct your profile, limit who sees your profile and records, withdraw consent by deleting the account, and complain to the data protection authority of your country.

## Security

HTTPS only, access restricted by Firestore and Storage rules, critical operations run on the server, videos are available only through temporary links.

## Changes

We will announce material changes in the app. The effective date is at the top of this document.
